Bhojan Chitta Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bhojan Chitta ("the App") handles information when you use its food, nutrition, and glucose-tracking features. The App is operated by its independent developer.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, the App may process your phone number for sign-in and verification; profile information such as your name, date of birth, sex, height, weight, activity level and goals; and food entries, nutrition logs, glucose readings and other information you choose to enter. Authentication also involves an account identifier.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, maintain their profiles, display and calculate food and nutrition tracking information, and provide requested App functionality. The App does not currently include a dedicated advertising or analytics integration. If the App's practices change, this policy will be updated.

Service providers and storage

The App uses Supabase for authentication and backend data storage. Phone verification messages may be processed through Twilio as part of the authentication service. These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services. Network and technical information may also be handled by these services in accordance with their applicable policies.

Health-related information

Glucose, nutrition and related entries are used for personal tracking and informational calculations. The App is not intended to diagnose or treat medical conditions or replace professional medical advice.

Sharing and sale of information

Information may be processed by service providers to operate the App. The developer does not currently use the App to sell personal information or serve targeted advertising. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while your account is active or as needed to operate the App, meet legal obligations, and resolve security or service issues. You may request access to or deletion of your account information through the developer's privacy contact. Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed subject to applicable legal and technical requirements. An in-app account-deletion mechanism is being reviewed for release readiness.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, including authentication and provider security features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

The App is not designed for children to use independently. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact the developer to request review or deletion.

Your choices

You may choose which optional profile, food and glucose information to enter. You can stop using the App, and may request access to or deletion of your information through the privacy contact below.

Contact and requests

Privacy contact: The developer will publish a dedicated contact email here before the policy is used for App Store submission. Until then, do not submit sensitive personal information through public GitHub issues.

Changes

We may update this policy when features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated date above will reflect material revisions.
